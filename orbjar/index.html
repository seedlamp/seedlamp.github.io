Meet the Orblings

Tiny round travellers with big dreams. Drop them into the jar, match two of a kind and watch them grow, all the way to the Orb King.

Ten worlds, ten twists

Every world is home to one of the Orblings, and every world changes the rules of the jar. Learn them all on the way to the Crown Realm.

A boss in every world

Each world ends with a boss fight - and every boss has a trick of its own.

Dress up

Unlock new looks for the whole game, and see what the Orblings wear when the seasons change.

Play your way

Travel the Journey, go further in the Master Journey with special rewards, chase your best score in Endless, or take on a new Daily Challenge every day.

Collect and compete

Grow your collection, complete goals, climb the leaderboards and share your results with friends.

Music made for Orbjar

Original music that changes with the worlds and the looks.

Calm by design

No timers, no energy, no coins. Ads never interrupt a round, and one purchase removes them for good. Free to play, works offline, cloud save with Google Play Games.
