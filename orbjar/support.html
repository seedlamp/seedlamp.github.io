Orbjar support

Problems, questions and lost progress - handled by one person, usually within a couple of days.

{% include support-contact.html hint="Please include your phone model and Android or iOS version - it makes most problems obvious straight away." %}

I lost my progress

Android: Sign in to Google Play Games in the game (Settings → Cloud save). Progress is backed up to your Google account, so it returns on a new phone or after a reinstall.

iOS: Sign in under Settings → Cloud save. Your progress syncs through iCloud to all your iOS devices with the same Apple Account (iCloud must be on for Orbjar in the iOS Settings).

If cloud save was never switched on, the progress lived only on the old device and cannot be recovered.

I bought something and did not get it

Android: Purchases are tied to your Google account and are restored automatically when the game starts. Close the game fully and reopen it, signed in with the Google account you paid with.

iOS: Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game, signed in with the Apple Account you paid with.

Still missing? Email us with the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by the store directly.

Android: open your Google Play order history, find the purchase and choose "Report a problem". Within 48 hours it is usually automatic.

iOS: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and pick the purchase.

Too many ads

Your first 3 rounds are ad-free. After that a small banner sits under the jar, and a full-screen ad can come between rounds - never during one, at most every 3rd round and 2 minutes apart. "Remove Ads" (or the Everything Pack) removes the banner and full-screen ads permanently. Rewarded videos stay available for the bonuses, but you never have to watch one.

How do I change my ad consent?

Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game (shown in the EU, UK and Switzerland).

Android: you can also reset your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

iOS: whether apps may track you is in the iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Orbjar works the same either way.

I am not in the Ranking

Android: the world ranking comes from Google Play Games. Sign in in the game, and make your Play Games profile public (Google Play Games app → your profile → settings). Private profiles do not appear in public lists.

iOS: the world ranking comes from Game Center. Sign in (Ranking → SIGN IN) with your Apple Account.

A new score can take a few minutes to show.

A level seems impossible

Every level has been beaten without power-ups, though some take a few tries. Merge low, keep the biggest orbs on one side, and save SWAP, BOMB or SHAKE for when the jar gets tight. Retries are free and stars never block the Journey. If a level really seems broken, tell us its number and we will look at it.

Delete my data

The game keeps no personal data on any Seedlamp server. Local data goes when you uninstall.

Android: cloud save can be deleted in the Play Games app under Settings → Delete Play Games account data.

iOS: sign out of Game Center or hide your profile in iOS Settings → Game Center, and turn off iCloud for the game under Settings → your name → iCloud.
