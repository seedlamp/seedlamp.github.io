About the game

Meet the Orblings - tiny round travellers with big dreams. Drop them into the jar, match two of a kind and watch them grow, all the way to the Orb King.

Every world is home to one of the Orblings, and every world changes the rules of the jar. Each world ends with a boss fight, and every boss has a trick of its own. Players unlock new looks for the whole game, see what the Orblings wear when the seasons change, chase a best score in Endless or take on a new Daily Challenge every day.

Orbjar is calm by design: no timers, no energy, no coins. Ads never interrupt a round, and one purchase removes them for good. It works offline and saves to the cloud with Google Play Games.

Features

  • Ten worlds, each with its own twist on the jar
  • A boss fight at the end of every world
  • Journey, Master Journey, Endless and a Daily Challenge
  • New looks for the whole game and season outfits
  • Goals, a card collection and leaderboards
  • Original music that changes with the worlds and the looks
