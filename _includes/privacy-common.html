When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach (for example your device model or a screenshot). We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report, based on our legitimate interest in supporting our players. We keep the conversation only as long as needed to handle it. Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data relating to you, ask us to restrict its processing, object to processing, and withdraw any consent you gave. Write to {{ site.data.studio.email }}; we answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Czech supervisory authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, or with the authority in your own country.
