What makes it different

A journey, not just a scoreOver a hundred levels across worlds that change the rules - a narrow jar, floaty gravity, a boss at the end of every world.
No ads during playNothing interrupts a run. Full-screen ads appear only between rounds, and rarely.
A daily challengeThe same level for everyone in the world, every day, with a sticker album and streaks.
Five looksEmoji faces, fruit, sweets, creatures and planets, each with its own music. Unlock them by playing or buy them outright.
