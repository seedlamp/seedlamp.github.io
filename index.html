About

The name

A seed needs water and light to grow. Our logo is exactly that: a drop of water and light around a young seedling. That is how we think about our work: start small, give it care, let it grow.

How we work

A one-person studio. Games that start in a second, respect your time and never interrupt play with ads. No dark patterns, no endless grind for its own sake.
